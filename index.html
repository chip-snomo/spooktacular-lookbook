Practicalities

the boring parts that make the fun parts work

Saint Paul in late October

Evening temps typically sit in the 30s–40s°F. Whatever you pick has to survive a coat over it and a coat check. Capes and gowns pack beautifully; hoops, wings, and tall hats don't. Plan the outer layer, not just the costume.

Three hours on your feet

6–9pm, heavy hors d'oeuvres + cocktails = standing and mingling the whole time. Comfortable shoes for Stacy, breathable layers for Matthew. Gown hems should clear the floor by an inch — carrying a train through a cocktail hour is a chore, not a look.

Makeup that survives photos

You will be photographed at a wedding. Setting spray, matte where it matters, and a small touch-up kit in Stacy's bag. Test the look once before the night — especially pale/ghost foundations, which read very differently under camera flash.

Buy early, not late October

Halloween stock sells out and shipping gets dicey the last week of October. Aim to have everything in hand by Oct 17. Thrift runs are best done now while the seasonal racks are fully stocked.

Honorable Mentions

if none of the five feel right
  • Sam & Rosie (or Merry & Pippin) — the third LOTR tier: cozy Shire waistcoats and cloaks, all comfort, zero elegance. If the night turns cold, this is the warm-blooded option — and the leaf brooches carry the theme.
  • Beetlejuice & Lydia — if you two want chaotic instead of elegant. Stripes, though: hot, loud, recognizable.
  • Death & the archaeologist's daughter — Terry Pratchett's Death (black robe, scythe, BINKY the white horse jokes) with Ysabell. Deep-cut book humor; huge if the crowd knows Discworld, invisible if they don't.
  • Victorian ghost bride & groom — leaning all the way into "we are also getting married, eternally." Most romantic option; white gown in late October is a bold move.
